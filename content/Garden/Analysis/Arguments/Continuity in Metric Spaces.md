The definition of continuity in metric spaces is almost identical to the one in real analysis. Let's state it:
>[!defn] Definition (Continuity in Metric Spaces)
 >A function between the metric spaces $(X,d_{X}), (Y,d_{Y})$, namely $f:(X,d_{X})\to(Y,d_{y})$ is continuous if for every point $x\in X$ and every $\epsilon>0$ there exists $\delta>0$ such that for all $x'\in X$,
>$$
> d_{X}(x,x')<\delta \implies d_{Y}(f(x),f(x'))<\epsilon.
>$$
